## 📐 Each roadmap Structure

```
roadmaps/
├── README.md
└── <track>/
    ├── README.md          overview, levels and contributors
    └── <level>/
        ├── README.md      goal, duration, prerequisites and the week table
        └── week-NN.md     one page per week
```

- **Track page:** an overview of the track, its levels with their goals, and who created it.
- **Level page:** the level's goal, duration and prerequisites, plus a table linking to every week.
- **Week page:** topics, resources and an assignment, in that order. Weeks that share one block of material are one page, such as `week-01-03.md`.
- **Resources:** links are marked 🎥 video, 📄 article or documentation, and 🎓 course. Arabic resources carry an `Arabic` tag.

## 🪜 Levels

- **Entry:** the foundations, for learners starting from scratch.
- **Intermediate:** building real skills and applying them in projects.
- **Advanced:** professional-level topics and complete projects.

A few tracks are organized differently:

- **Backend** follows its own sections (Entry, Beginner, Intermediate 1 and 2, Advanced, Deployment & Production, AI for Developers), and each section keeps its own week numbers.
- **Data Science** runs its three levels in parallel over one 20-week season. Members follow the level that matches their skill and move up the next season.
- **Cyber Security** has an Entry level, then two specializations to choose from: SOC (Blue Team) or Penetration Testing (Red Team).
