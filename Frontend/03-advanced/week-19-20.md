### 🏗️ Project: E-commerce App
_Weeks 19-20 · Next.js + TypeScript_

- Product catalog with categories and search/filter.
- Product details page, cart, and a checkout flow (can be a mock checkout, no real payments needed).
- User authentication (sign up / log in), with a protected "My Orders" page.
- Backend: **Prisma + PostgreSQL** for products, users, and orders. Fetch/cache product and order data with **TanStack Query**; keep cart state (items, quantities, open/closed drawer) in **Zustand**.
- Typed with TypeScript throughout; deployed to Vercel.

> 💡 **Stack note:** across these three projects, prefer **TanStack Query** for anything that comes from a server (fetching, caching, refetching) instead of raw `useEffect` + `fetch`, and a small store like **Zustand** for client-only state (UI toggles, filters, cart drawer open/closed) instead of always reaching for Redux. For any project that needs its own backend/database, use **Prisma + PostgreSQL** — it's what most real companies use, and it teaches you schemas, migrations, and type-safe queries.

> 💡 For all three advanced projects: write at least a few tests with Jest/RTL, add basic performance optimizations (lazy loading, memoization), and deploy the final version — these are the projects you'll actually show employers.
