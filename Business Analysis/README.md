## 📖 Overview

A 15-week business roadmap, in three progressive levels, that takes you from fundamentals to mastery. You start with entrepreneurship basics (understanding startups, defining real problems and generating ideas), move on to market research, the Business Model Canvas and pitching, and finish with business analysis: analyzing, prioritizing, modeling and documenting requirements (diagrams, BPMN, BRD/SRS, user stories, use cases and acceptance criteria) and delivering them to the team. Each level builds on the skills of the one before it. Suitable for beginners with no prior business background.

**Learning path:** 🌱 Entry → 🚀 Intermediate → 🏆 Advanced  
**Total duration:** 15 weeks

## 🗂️ Levels

| Level | Weeks | Goal |
|:------|:------|:-----|
| 🌱 [Entry](01-entry/README.md) | 3 | Build core foundations: understand entrepreneurship, identify and define real problems, and generate and select ideas to validate. |
| 🚀 [Intermediate](02-intermediate/README.md) | 4 | Deepen skills and apply concepts: research a market, build a Business Model Canvas, and pitch your idea. |
| 🏆 [Advanced](03-advanced/README.md) | 8 | Master advanced concepts and real-world projects: analyze, prioritize, model, document and validate business requirements, and hand them over to the development team. |
