## 📖 Overview

The Data Science track curriculum of MEGA Team MU. Every topic is paired with curated learning material (in English and, where a reliable one exists, in Arabic) and a hands-on project built on a real, named dataset (Kaggle / UCI / open sources), so that anyone who completes a level can genuinely apply what they learned, not just recognize the terminology.

**How a season works:** a season runs for 20 weeks, and all three levels (Entry, Intermediate and Advanced) run in parallel for the same 20 weeks, each with its own members and mentors. A member is placed in the level that matches their current skill, follows that level's 20-week plan for the whole season, and moves up to the next level in the following season.

**Core principles:**
- Every week has one assigned English playlist/course, one Arabic explanation playlist where available, and one end-of-week project on real data.
- Entry does not stop at data cleaning and visualization: it closes with an introduction to Machine Learning (Linear and Logistic Regression).
- Each level ends with a Capstone Project and an Exit Check, a short list of "can you do this, unprompted?" items.

**Time commitment:** about 7-9 hours per week at every level (roughly 2-3 hours on the assigned material, 4-5 hours on the end-of-week project, plus review time during Capstone weeks).

**Learning path:** 🌱 Entry → 🚀 Intermediate → 🏆 Advanced (one level per season)  
**Duration:** 20 weeks per level

## 🗂️ Levels

| Level | Weeks | Goal |
|:------|:------|:-----|
| 🌱 [Entry](01-entry/README.md) | 20 | By the end of this level, every member can write working Python code, understand basic statistics, query with SQL, clean and visualize data, and train a basic Machine Learning model — plus has a clear, informed picture of the other specializations under the Data Science umbrella. |
| 🚀 [Intermediate](02-intermediate/README.md) | 20 | Real depth in Machine Learning — training, evaluating, and improving models — plus a guided "taste" of every sub-specialization (NLP, Computer Vision, Data Engineering, MLOps) so that choosing a specialization at the Advanced level is based on real hands-on experience, not guesswork. |
| 🏆 [Advanced](03-advanced/README.md) | 20 | True mastery, not another round of "taste." Weeks 1–6 are shared by every advanced member; from Week 7 onward, each member follows the track they chose based on real experience from the Intermediate level's taste weeks. |
