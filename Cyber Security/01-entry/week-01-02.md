# 📘 Weeks 01-02 · Networks

> **Track:** Cyber Security  ·  **Level:** 🌱 Entry

## 📚 Topics
1. Networking Fundamentals
2. Networking for Security

_Learn the fundamentals of computer networks and understand how network communication works from a cybersecurity perspective._

> 💡 **Important:** Understand the difference between a VPN and a Proxy instead of just memorizing the definitions.
