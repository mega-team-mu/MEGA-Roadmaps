# 📘 Week 03 · Clean Architecture

> **Track:** Backend  ·  **Level:** 🏆 Advanced

## 📚 Topics
1. Clean Architecture & Separation of Concerns

> 💡 Focus on understanding why applications are divided into layers, what each layer is responsible for, and how dependencies should flow between them.
>
> The goal is to build applications that are easier to maintain, test, and extend. Don't add unnecessary layers or patterns just to follow an architectural template.
