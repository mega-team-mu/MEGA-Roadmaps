# 📘 Week 11 · Health Checks

> **Track:** Backend  ·  **Level:** 🏆 Advanced

## 📚 Topics
1. What Are Health Checks?

> 💡 Focus on understanding how Health Checks help monitor application availability and the health of important dependencies such as databases and external services.
>
> Start by creating a `/health` endpoint, then add a database health check and understand how the application reports healthy and unhealthy states.
