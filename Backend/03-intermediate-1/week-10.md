Solve the exercises related to:

- **Custom Types:** Query collections of objects based on their properties.
- **Grouping:** Group customers by bank and calculate how many customers meet a specific condition in each group.
- **Joining:** Join customers with banks to retrieve related information.
- **Combined Queries:** Combine filtering, sorting, grouping, and projection to solve more complex problems.
