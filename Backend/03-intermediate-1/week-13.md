## 📝 Assignment
- [ ] Design Patterns Mini Task

Choose **one Design Pattern** from the studied resources and implement a small example in C#.

- Choose one pattern and implement a simple scenario where it can be used.
- Explain what problem the pattern solves and why you chose it.
- Demonstrate how your implementation works.

You can choose any pattern covered in the resources, such as **Singleton, Adapter, Strategy, or Factory**.
