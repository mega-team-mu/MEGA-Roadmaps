## 📝 Assignment
- [ ] Shopping System

The **Shopping System** is a simple console-based application built using **C#** that allows users to manage their shopping experience efficiently. It  provides essential features such as adding items to a cart, removing them, and checking out. Additionally, users can undo their last action and view the total price before completing their purchase.

**Core Features:**

1. **Add Items to Cart** – Users can browse products and add them to their cart.
2. **View Cart** – Displays all items currently in the cart.
3. **Remove Items** – Allows users to remove specific products from their cart.
4. **Checkout** – Finalizes the purchase and displays an order summary.
5. **Undo Last Action** – Users can undo their most recent action (add/remove).
6. **View Total Price** – Shows the total price of all items before checkout.
7. **Exit** – Users can exit the system at any time.
